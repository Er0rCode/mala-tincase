Hi there! I’m Mala—though you can also call me Lala, Amala, or 'Tung', as my grandma lovingly calls me!

I’m a cancer girl who loves all things PINK and anything dipped in cozy pastel shades. When I’m not sketching or drawing, you’ll probably find me listening to my favorite songs by Sabrina Carpenter and Taylor Swift or getting completely lost in a good book. Speaking of reading, I have my top 3 absolute recommendations for you! My all-time favorite is Hujan by Tere Liye, followed by the beautiful Laut Bercerita by Leila S. Chudori, and the deeply touching Daun Yang Jatuh Tak Pernah Membenci Angin, also by Tere Liye.

I run on a delicious mix of spicy seblak, nasi padang, yayah’s chicken soup, cherry tomatoes, instant noodles, and—most importantly—lots of POTATOES & CHEESE! I usually pair all of that with a cup of MATCHA, berry juice, full cream milk, or a classic Milo!
